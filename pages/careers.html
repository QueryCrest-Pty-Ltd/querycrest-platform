Internship Programme

Join the QueryCrest Team

Gain real-world industry experience at a growing South African EdTech platform. We are looking for motivated students ready to apply their skills and make a genuine impact.

About the Internship

QueryCrest is a South African EdTech platform helping students navigate university applications, bursaries, and student life. Our internship programme gives students hands-on, industry-standard experience on a product used by thousands of real students across the country.

You will not be doing coffee runs or sitting in orientation sessions. From day one you will work on real features, real content, and real challenges — with direct mentorship from our core team.

This is a Work Integrated Learning (WIL) internship placement. Applicants must be registered for WIL through their institution to be eligible.

Open Internship Roles

What to Expect

We are a lean, mission-driven team that genuinely cares about the students we serve. You will have real ownership over your work and direct exposure to how a tech product is built and grown.

  • Work on a live product used by thousands of South African students
  • Remote-first — work from anywhere in South Africa
  • Direct mentorship and regular feedback from the core team
  • Flexible working hours that fit around your academic schedule
  • Industry experience that directly supports your WIL academic requirements
  • Reference letter and full internship documentation upon completion
No corporate bureaucracy. No endless meetings. Just meaningful work with a supportive, small team.

Documents you'll likely need

External bursary providers commonly require:

  • South African ID document (Certified)
  • Latest academic results (Certified)
  • Parent(s) or guardian(s) ID documents (Certified)
  • Proof of university acceptance or registration (where applicable)
  • CV (required by some bursaries)
  • Proof of residence (Certified)
  • Parent(s) or guardian(s) bank statements
  • Proof of unemployment, SASSA grant confirmation, or payslip
  • Motivational letter (required by some bursaries)

These are commonly required by external bursary providers. Please ensure they're uploaded in the Documents section of your QueryCrest profile before submitting.
